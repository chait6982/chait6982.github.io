RAG · Retrieval & Search

Smart Document Q&A System

What it is

An AI system that answers questions about a document by finding the right passages first, then writing a grounded answer — and it decides on its own whether to search the document, search the web, or answer directly.

What it is used for

Letting a person ask plain questions of a large document (a report, contract, or manual) and get accurate, sourced answers instead of reading the whole thing.

Why it stands out

I did not just build it — I measured it. Using an industry evaluation tool (RAGAS), the answers scored 97% on faithfulness (staying true to the source).

✓ 0.97 faithfulness · 0.92 precision
PythonChroma vector databaseEmbeddingsRAGAS evaluation
Where it applies

Insurance & finance: policy wordings, claim files, regulatory rulebooks. Pharmaceutical & medical: trial protocols, drug monographs and clinical guidelines, as a research aid with a qualified person reviewing every answer. Government & defence: legislation, procurement papers, technical manuals. Environment: long scientific and impact reports. Because answers are scored for faithfulness to the source, it suits regulated settings where an unsupported answer carries real risk.

Agent Infrastructure · Containerised

Market Intelligence Tool Server

What it is

A tool server that gives any AI agent live capabilities — company overview, competitor list, product portfolio, pricing snapshot and news pulse — using MCP, the emerging open standard for connecting AI to external systems.

What it is used for

Letting an AI assistant reach real, current information from outside itself, instead of relying only on what it learned during training.

Why it stands out
